# ADR-0028: EU data-intermediary stance and minimisation against compulsion

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Principles:** P5, P6, P7

## Context
Partner analysis of the Data Governance Act (2022/868) and Data Act (2023/2854). The Digital Omnibus would fold the DGA into the Data Act with voluntary registration; Council mandate June 2026, trilogues ongoing.

## Decision
- Register the broker as a data intermediation service for civil defence and humanitarian tenants; service fees only; no use of data for own purposes.
- Military tenant outside these rules (national defence law).
- **Hold as little as possible** so a Data Act exceptional-need request returns little.
- **Access log:** publish only fingerprints in the transparency log; full receipts encrypted per tenant (who read whom, where, is sensitive).
- **Humanitarian data:** partner-held records and wearer-held keys so Hexatech cannot hand it over.
- Functional separation now; decide on a separate legal entity (e.g. HEXA Data) once the Omnibus settles.

## Consequences
Neutrality becomes a sellable property. Confirm with counsel before structuring the company.

## Alternatives considered
Ignore intermediary status (loses trust label); single entity regardless (may conflict with DGA while it applies).
