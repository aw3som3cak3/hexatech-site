# ADR-0029: MVP scope and deliberate deferrals

- **Status:** Accepted (amended by ADR-0034)
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Principles:** P1, P4

## Context
Nobody pays for brokers or national roots before market pull, format usability and offline reads are proven.

## Decision
See docs/mvp.md. Stage 0: mock medic screen + stopwatch test. Stage 1: JavaCard demonstrator, Android medic app, owner app, test CA, real transparency log. Deferred: Tier 2/TEE, attestations, EUDI, national roots, FIDO2, iOS, certification, production hardware.

## Consequences
Exit criterion: full offline flow, captured phone dies within a day, one letter of intent or funded pilot.

## Alternatives considered
Build the full architecture first.
