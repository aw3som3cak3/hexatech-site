# ADR-0030: Sensor pockets optional, not core

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Principles:** P8

## Context
Partner v0.1 added a passive dosimeter and chemical indicator in the back plate.

## Decision
Option pockets remain in the mechanical design; sensors are customer-specified extras, not part of the base product or its certification scope.

## Consequences
Keeps the first product focused; avoids extra regulatory scope.

## Alternatives considered
Standard sensors in every tag.
