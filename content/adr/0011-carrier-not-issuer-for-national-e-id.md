# ADR-0011: Carrier, not issuer, for national e-ID

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Principles:** P6

## Context
Sweden launches state e-ID Sverige-id on 1 Dec 2026 (Polisen, LOA4); competing with the state and BankID is futile. Globally, enrollment/proofing is the hard, politically owned part.

## Decision
Position as a **carrier**: alternative bearer for national e-IDs, external secure device for EUDI Wallets, and carrier for enrollment systems such as MOSIP in identity-poor markets.

## Consequences
Sell to issuers (long cycles, large contracts). Avoid becoming a regulated identity provider.

## Alternatives considered
Issue our own national-grade identity (regulatory burden, competes with states).
